Internal — Brand System

The UltraWrap
identity system.

Positioning, logo, palette, typography and application rules. This page is the working reference for anyone producing UltraWrap Studio materials.

Positioning

Luxury automotive transformation.

UltraWrap Studio doesn't "put vinyl on cars" — it transforms the appearance and personality of a vehicle. Primary tagline: "Your car. Your color. Your signature." Brand essence line (footer, lockups, vehicle): "The art of automotive transformation." Voice: confident, sophisticated, precise, minimal. Never cheesy, never salesy, never an exclamation mark where a period will do.

Logo

Wordmark + UW monogram.

The monogram merges U and W into one angular gesture — the U in ivory, the W in champagne gold, sharing a baseline like two panels meeting at a seam. It survives one-color reproduction, embroidery, die-cut decals and a 16px favicon.

Application

On screen, on vehicle,
on everything.

Buttons

Primary — Gold Secondary — Ghost

Rectangular, 2px radius, letterspaced caps. One gold button per view section — gold loses its authority when it's everywhere.

Photography direction

Low-key studio lighting, single warm key light, deep shadow, 3/4 and profile angles, gold rim light on body lines. Editorial, not inventory. Detail close-ups of edges and seams are brand assets — shoot them on every build.

Vehicle branding concept

Following the original studio Tesla: full satin olive body, champagne gold door lockup (wordmark + services + URL), gold "Certified Wrapped" style badge on the fender. Restraint is the message — the wrap itself is the advertisement.

Social profile concept

Avatar: gold one-color monogram on obsidian. Grid rhythm: hero shot → detail close-up → before/after. Story highlights in kicker-style gold caps: BUILDS · PROCESS · TESLA · PPF. Handle placeholder: [INSTAGRAM URL].

Icon style

1.5px stroke line icons, geometric, no fills, gold on dark. Never emoji, never filled blob icons.

Don'ts

No gold fields or gold backgrounds. No condensed free fonts. No drop shadows on the logo. No price-led messaging ("cheap", "best price", "discount"). No exclamation marks in headlines.
