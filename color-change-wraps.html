Good to Know

Straight answers.

Will a wrap protect my paint? +

A wrap covers the paint and shields it from light surface wear, and on healthy factory paint a professionally installed and removed wrap is designed to come off cleanly. For dedicated impact protection, see paint protection film.

How long does a full color change take? +

Typically several days from drop-off to delivery depending on the vehicle, condition and finish. We confirm your timeline before work begins — and we don't rush the parts that matter.

What does it cost? +

Every vehicle is different: size, condition, coverage, material, finish, complexity and any existing vinyl removal all factor in. Request a custom quote and we'll give you a real number for your exact car.
