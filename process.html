Our Process

Nine steps.
Zero shortcuts.

From the first conversation to the day you drive away — and the years after — here is exactly what working with UltraWrap Studio looks like.

01 — Discover

You find us

Through a car you saw on the road, a build on social, or a search that finally surfaced a studio instead of a sign shop.

02 — Consult

We talk

Call the studio or request a quote. A specialist — not a call center — discusses your vehicle, your goals and your options. Design consultation is free.

03 — Design

The vision takes shape

Colors, finishes and direction are developed. Custom projects receive a professional digital mockup on your vehicle.

04 — Approve

You sign off

Concept, coverage, materials and timeline — agreed in full before anything begins. No surprises, ever.

05 — Prepare

The vehicle is readied

Deep decontamination, paint condition documented, trim addressed. Preparation is where most wrap failures are born — so it's where we spend the unglamorous hours.

06 — Install

Precision installation

Premium film, controlled environment, edges wrapped where appropriate, contours and difficult areas treated as the craft — not the compromise.

07 — Inspect

Final quality inspection

Every panel, every edge, every seam reviewed under studio lighting before you're ever called.

08 — Deliver

The reveal

You walk around a different vehicle. We walk you through the work, the warranty, and everything we did.

09 — Protect

Care for the road ahead

You leave with clear care guidance and a studio that picks up the phone whenever you have a question.

Care & Maintenance

Keep it looking installed-yesterday.

General guidance for wrapped vehicles — always follow the specific care recommendations of the film manufacturer on your vehicle.

Hand wash

Hand washing with a pH-neutral automotive shampoo and soft microfiber is the gold standard. Skip brush-style automatic car washes — they're abrasive to any finish, wrapped or not.

Mind the chemicals

Avoid harsh solvents, aggressive degreasers and abrasive polishes on vinyl. If a product doesn't say it's safe for wraps, assume it isn't until confirmed.

Act on contaminants

Bird droppings, tree sap and fuel spills should come off promptly with gentle cleaning — the longer they sit, the harder they are on any finish.

Pressure washing

If you pressure wash, keep sensible distance and angle, and stay away from edges and seams. When in doubt, hand wash.

Dry gently

Soft microfiber or filtered forced air. Avoid dragging dirt across the surface with a dirty towel.

Think long-term

Parking under cover when possible and washing regularly extends the life of every finish. Ask us about ceramic coating over wrap for easier upkeep.
