What It Does

A finish that keeps
its edge.

A ceramic coating is a liquid-applied protective layer that bonds to the surface, adding hardness, hydrophobic behavior and a noticeably deeper gloss. Water beads and sheets off, contaminants release more easily, and wash days get dramatically shorter.

  • Deeper, glassier gloss on paint, wrap or PPF
  • Hydrophobic surface — water and grime let go
  • Easier, safer maintenance washing
  • Helps guard against UV and environmental fallout

Pairings

Better together.

Over Fresh Paint

Lock in a flawless finish on a new delivery or freshly corrected paint.

Over a New Wrap

Coating a wrap helps it shed contaminants and keeps satin and matte finishes cleaner between washes.

Over PPF

The complete stack: film for impact protection, coating for gloss and easy care.

Product performance and durability vary by coating system and care — we'll review the manufacturer's documentation for the specific product used on your vehicle rather than quoting blanket numbers.
