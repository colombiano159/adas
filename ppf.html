Why PPF

Protection you'll forget is there.

Paint protection film is a clear, durable urethane layer installed over painted surfaces to help defend against rock chips, road debris and everyday wear. Done properly, it disappears into the car — no visible edges, no distortion, no drama.

It's the difference between driving your car and worrying about it.

Over Wrap or Paint

The Difference

Installation is everything.

PPF is unforgiving of shortcuts. Our installations follow the same studio standard as our wraps: full decontamination, precise patterning, wrapped edges where appropriate, and a panel-by-panel inspection before delivery — backed by our 1-year workmanship warranty. For film-specific performance and coverage terms, we'll walk you through the manufacturer's documentation for the exact product on your vehicle.
