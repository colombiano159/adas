Accent & Detail Services

Precision, piece by piece.

Not every transformation needs full coverage. These services change how a vehicle reads — with restraint.

Partial Wraps

Hood, roof, mirrors, trunk, fender and door accents, racing-inspired sections — contrast where it counts, integrated with the vehicle's lines.

Chrome Delete

Factory chrome trim transformed into gloss black, satin black or body-matched finishes for a cleaner, more modern stance. A Tesla and luxury-sedan favorite.

Racing Stripes

Single, dual, offset or fully custom stripe designs and motorsport-inspired graphics — measured, centered and aligned to the millimeter.

Hood Wraps

Carbon-fiber appearance, matte, satin, gloss, contrasting colors or custom graphics. A single panel that changes the whole front end.

Decals & Custom Graphics

Logos, numbers, names, racing graphics and personalized designs — produced from premium cast films and installed clean.

Headlight & Tail Light Tint

Subtle smoked or tinted lenses as a cosmetic customization, where legally appropriate. We'll advise on what's suitable for road use in your jurisdiction.

Interior Wraps

The view from the driver's seat.

Dash trim, center console, door trim — refinished in carbon-fiber-style, gloss, matte, satin or custom finishes. The interior is where you actually live with the car; it should match the outside.
