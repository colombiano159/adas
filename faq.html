Cost & Timing

How much does a wrap cost? +

Every vehicle is different, so we don't publish flat prices. Cost depends on vehicle size and condition, coverage, material and finish, design complexity, removal of any existing vinyl, and installation requirements. Request a custom quote — you'll get a real number for your exact vehicle, not a range designed to get you in the door.

How long does a wrap take? +

Most full color changes take several days from drop-off to delivery; partial work is often faster. Complexity, condition and finish all matter. We confirm your timeline before work begins — and we won't rush the preparation or the difficult panels to hit a date.

How long will a wrap last? +

Lifespan depends on the film, finish, climate, storage and care — a garaged, hand-washed vehicle keeps its wrap looking new far longer than one that lives outside. We'll set honest expectations for the specific film and finish you choose, based on the manufacturer's own documentation.

Paint & Vehicle Condition

Can a wrap damage my paint? +

On healthy factory paint, professional-grade vinyl that is properly installed and properly removed is designed to come off cleanly. Resprayed panels, aftermarket paint or pre-existing damage can behave differently at removal — which is why we inspect and document your paint before wrapping and tell you exactly what we find.

What if my vehicle already has paint damage? +

Vinyl conforms to the surface underneath, so chips, scratches and dents can still telegraph through a wrap. We'll flag anything that should be addressed first, and anything we can safely work around. No surprises after installation.

Do you remove old wraps? +

Yes. We remove existing vinyl — ours or anyone else's — as a standalone service or as part of a new wrap. Removal effort varies with the film's age and quality, which is one of the factors in your quote.

Design & Options

What finishes are available? +

Gloss, satin, matte, metallic, color-shift, carbon-fiber texture, brushed and other special-effect finishes — hundreds of options across premium film lines, plus fully custom printed vinyl. Final selection happens in the studio with physical samples on your vehicle.

Can I design my own graphics? +

Absolutely — that's what Design Your Vision is for. Bring an idea, artwork or references; our designers develop it into a professional digital mockup on your vehicle, and nothing is produced until you approve it.

Do you wrap interiors, motorcycles or boats? +

Yes — interior trim, motorcycles and boats each have a dedicated specialty program. See Specialty Wraps.

Tesla & Specialty

Do you work on Tesla vehicles? +

Constantly. Model 3, Model Y, Model S and Model X are among the most-wrapped vehicles in the studio — full color changes, chrome deletes, PPF and interior trim.

Do you wrap Cybertrucks? +

Yes — the Cybertruck has its own dedicated program. Its flat stainless panels demand precise, planned installation, and reward it.

Do you work on exotics? +

Exotic and luxury vehicles are the studio's focus. Complex aero, carbon components and delicate finishes get disassembly-informed installation and the patience they demand.

Ownership & Practicalities

Can you wrap a leased vehicle? +

Many clients wrap leased vehicles and have the wrap removed before return. Review your lease terms first — we're happy to talk through what's typical, but your agreement governs.

How does a wrap affect insurance or registration? +

Requirements vary by state and insurer — some ask you to note a color change. We recommend a quick call to your insurer and DMV; we can't provide legal or insurance advice, but we can tell you what our clients commonly encounter.

Is headlight or tail light tint legal? +

Light tint laws vary by jurisdiction, and some tints are not road-legal in some places. We present light tint as a cosmetic customization and will advise on suitability where we can — checking your local regulations is essential.

What warranty do you offer? +

Our installations carry a 1-year workmanship warranty, in writing. Film performance itself is covered by each manufacturer's own warranty documentation, which we review with you for the exact product on your vehicle.

How should I maintain my wrap? +

Hand wash with pH-neutral shampoo, avoid harsh chemicals and brush-style car washes, deal with bird droppings promptly, and follow the film manufacturer's care recommendations. Full guidance lives on our care page.

How do I request a quote? +

Use the custom quote form — tell us your vehicle, the service you're considering and your vision, attach photos, and we'll come back with a tailored quote. Or simply call the studio.
