About the Studio

A studio,
not a shop.

UltraWrap Studio exists for one kind of client: the driver who notices everything.

The Idea

Wraps, treated like
they matter.

UltraWrap Studio was built in North Bergen, New Jersey on a simple observation: the people who love their cars most had nowhere to take them. Sign shops treated wraps as big stickers. Detail chains treated them as an upsell. Nobody treated them as what they are — the single most transformative thing you can do to a vehicle's appearance.

So we built the studio we wished existed. Premium film only. Preparation that borders on obsessive. Edges wrapped where appropriate. A final inspection under lights that forgive nothing. And a 1-year warranty that means we stand behind every panel.

What We Believe

The details are the job.

Specialization wins

We don't do fleet graphics or commercial branding. Luxury vehicles, enthusiasts and premium customization — that focus is why the work is exceptional.

Experience shows

Years of hands-on wrap experience across luxury, exotic and electric vehicles — visible in every edge we finish.

Personal service

You talk to the people who touch your car. Boutique means your project has a name, not a ticket number.

Who We Serve

If you notice the details,
you're one of us.

Porsche, Lamborghini, Ferrari, McLaren, Mercedes-AMG, BMW, Range Rover, Audi, Bentley, Aston Martin, Maserati, Cadillac, Lucid, Rivian — and a special place for Tesla and Cybertruck owners, the most passionate customizers on the road. If you care how your vehicle looks, it belongs here regardless of the badge.
